Bio

Bibiana Riet is a Brazilian-Uruguayan actress based in Brazil, with a background in theatre, dance and physical performance. Her stage experience includes character-driven comedy and movement-based productions.

Her studies in psychology have shaped her curiosity about human behavior and the motivations behind people's actions. She is drawn to characters with complex inner lives, from unconventional and unpredictable personalities to those navigating vulnerability, resilience and extraordinary circumstances.

Alongside acting, Bibiana has experience in adaptation writing and continues to develop her work for camera, pursuing opportunities in Brazil and internationally.

ARTISTIC APPROACH

Characters who live
in the in-between.

What I love most about acting is the freedom to become someone entirely different from myself — to explore unfamiliar lives, unexpected choices and the contradictions that make people human.

Whether through comedy or drama, I'm drawn to the possibilities of transformation and to stories that make audiences feel, question and connect. I hope to bring characters to life in ways that stay with people, offering a new perspective, a moment of recognition or simply something meaningful to carry with them.
