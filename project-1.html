ABOUT THE WORK

‘Xandú e seus causos’ is a theatre production based on a collective adaptation of ‘A Farsa, com Cangaceiro, Truco e Padre’, developed collaboratively by the class and their instructor. In the production, I played Xandú Quaresma.
